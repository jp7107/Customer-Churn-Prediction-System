## 1. Project Background & Executive Overview

Customer churn represents one of the single most damaging sources of revenue erosion in telecommunications. Acquiring a new customer is estimated to cost between 5 and 25 times more than retaining an existing customer.

This project delivers a machine learning classification system built on the **IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset** (7,043 customer accounts). The primary objective is to reliably identify customers at imminent risk of terminating service, while maximizing recall (capturing true churners) and preserving sufficient precision to make proactive retention interventions economically viable.

The complete system encompasses:
1. Leakage-safe preprocessing and domain-grounded feature engineering.
2. Comparative benchmarking across **Logistic Regression**, **Random Forest**, and **XGBoost**.
3. Stratified 5-Fold Cross Validation and training-only threshold optimization.
4. Final evaluation on an untouched holdout test partition.
5. High-throughput REST API deployed with FastAPI.

## 2. Dataset Understanding & Exploratory Insights

The dataset comprises **7,043 records** and **21 columns**:

- **Target Variable (`Churn`):** 5,174 "No" (73.46%) vs. 1,869 "Yes" (26.54%).
- **Imbalance Ratio:** ~2.77:1 negative-to-positive ratio.
- **Key Predictors:**
  - Demographics: `gender`, `SeniorCitizen`, `Partner`, `Dependents`
  - Account Information: `tenure`, `Contract`, `PaperlessBilling`, `PaymentMethod`, `MonthlyCharges`, `TotalCharges`
  - Subscribed Services: `PhoneService`, `MultipleLines`, `InternetService`, `OnlineSecurity`, `OnlineBackup`, `DeviceProtection`, `TechSupport`, `StreamingTV`, `StreamingMovies`

### Critical Preprocessing Findings
1. **Missing Data in `TotalCharges`:** Standard pandas scans report 0 missing values because blank spaces (`" "`) are present in 11 records. All 11 instances occur when `tenure == 0`. Our pipeline applies a deterministic rule: if `TotalCharges` is blank and `tenure == 0`, it is set to `0.0`.
2. **Special Non-Missing Categories:** Values such as `"No internet service"` and `"No phone service"` carry distinct operational meaning and are encoded directly using `OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown="ignore")`.
3. **`customerID` Exclusion:** Customer ID strings are strictly excluded from predictive features to prevent artificial overfitting.

## 3. Machine Learning Architecture & Leakage Safeguards

To ensure that reported metrics reflect genuine out-of-sample generalization:
1. **Stratified Holdout Split:** 80% (5,634 records) train partition vs. 20% (1,409 records) untouched test partition.
2. **Encapsulated Preprocessing:** All encoders, median imputers, and scalers are embedded in Scikit-Learn `Pipeline` and `ColumnTransformer` instances. They are fitted **strictly on training folds** during cross-validation, and never on the full dataset.
3. **Feature Engineering Parity:** Engineered features (`service_count`, `tenure_group`) are generated by a Scikit-Learn transformer (`TelcoFeatureEngineer`) shared across training and API serving.

## 5. Decision Threshold Optimization

Using out-of-fold cross-validation predictions on the training data, we analyzed F1-score as a function of the classification threshold:
- At $\tau = 0.50$ (default): F1 = 0.6253, Recall = 79.41%, Precision = 51.56%.
- At $\tau = 0.54$ (optimal): F1 = **0.6281**, Recall = **77.01%**, Precision = **53.04%**.

This optimal threshold of **0.54** was frozen into the model pipeline metadata and applied during test evaluation and API serving.

## 7. Business & Operational Interpretation

In churn prevention, a **False Negative** (a customer who churns unnoticed) results in permanent lost customer lifetime value (LTV). Conversely, a **False Positive** (a customer who stays but receives a retention promotion or discount) incurs modest outreach and concession costs. 

Because the cost of losing a customer typically dwarfs the cost of a promotional email or incentive, a model with **77% recall** provides vastly superior business value over an uncalibrated default model that catches only 50% of churners.

## 8. Limitations & Future Roadmap

1. **Absence of Longitudinal Telemetry:** Current data represents a static snapshot. Real-world telecom systems benefit from time-series logs of call drops, latency spikes, and usage decline over rolling 7/14/30-day windows.
2. **Probability Calibration:** While the ranking performance (ROC-AUC 0.848) is robust, applying Platt scaling or Isotonic Regression on a dedicated validation split would yield even more calibrated probability estimates.
3. **Automated Retraining & Drift Detection:** Deploying Prometheus/Grafana or Evidently AI to monitor feature distribution shift (Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistics) and model prediction decay in production.
